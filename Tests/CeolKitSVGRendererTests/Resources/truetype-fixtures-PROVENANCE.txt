TrueType fixtures — provenance
==============================

Files:     CeolKitTest-Short.ttf, CeolKitTest-Long.ttf, CeolKitTest.ttc,
           truetype-reference.json
Generator: truetype-fixtures.py (this directory), with fontTools 4.63.0
Licence:   CeolKit's own. Every outline is drawn by the script for the tests; no third-party
           font data is included.

Why they exist
--------------
`TrueTypeOutlineTests` (issue #189) checks `OpenTypeFont`'s reading of TrueType `glyf`
outlines and font collections against fontTools' reading of the same bytes, recorded in
truetype-reference.json. The glyphs cover what the parser has to get right: on-curve-only
contours, runs of off-curve points with implied midpoints, a contour that starts off-curve,
one with no on-curve point at all, composites with byte and word offsets, each of the three
transform forms, a nested composite, and a point-matched component. Both `loca` formats are
covered, and the collection's two faces differ in PostScript name and `OS/2.fsType`.

To regenerate, run `python3 truetype-fixtures.py` here and commit all four outputs together.

These are test resources only, never shipped to consumers of the package.
